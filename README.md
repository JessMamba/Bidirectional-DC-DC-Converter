Three-Stage Cubic Bidirectional DC-DC ConverterA high-gain, non-isolated three-stage cubic bidirectional DC-DC power converter optimized for renewable energy interfaces, battery storage systems, and electric vehicle (V2G/G2V) applications. The topology achieves ultra-high voltage transfer ratios ($1/(1-D)^3$ boost gain and $D^3$ buck conversion) with reduced switch stress, maintaining continuous conduction mode (CCM) across operating ranges.  Technical SpecificationsRated Power: 900 W  Voltage Conversion: 24 V (Low-Voltage Side) $\leftrightarrow$ 300 V (High-Voltage Side)  Switching Frequency: 50 kHz  Peak Efficiency: 94.16%  Topologies & Gain:Step-Up (Boost Mode): $M_{CCM} = \frac{1}{(1-D)^3}$ at $D \approx 0.57$  Step-Down (Buck Mode): $M_{CCM} = D^3$ at $D \approx 0.43$  Grounding: Common ground configuration across low and high voltage sides  Hardware & Design HighlightsActive Switches: 6 MOSFETs  High-Voltage Side ($S_1, S_5, S_6$): SRC65R032FB Super Junction N-Channel (650V, 88A)  Intermediate Stage ($S_2, S_3, S_4$): IRFP4227PBF (200V, 65A)  Passive Components: 3 Inductors ($330\ \mu\text{H}$ nominal) and 4 Capacitors ($C_1, C_2 = 110\ \mu\text{F}$, $C_L, C_H = 470\ \mu\text{F}$)  Control Strategy: Mode-Dependent PID Feedback Controller designed for minimal overshoot ($<2.5\%$) and rapid zero steady-state error regulation in both operating modes.
